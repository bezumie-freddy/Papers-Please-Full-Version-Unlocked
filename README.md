## Overview
<a id="overview"></a>

*Papers, Please* is an immersive narrative-driven game where players assume the role of an immigration officer in the fictional totalitarian state of Arstotzka. Set in the year 1982, you will be tasked with managing the flow of immigrants at the border, scrutinizing their documents to prevent the entry of potential threats such as terrorists and smugglers. This title stands out in the adventure genre for its unique premise, compelling story, and moral dilemmas that challenge players to navigate the complexities of duty versus empathy, making it an experience unlike typical alternatives.
